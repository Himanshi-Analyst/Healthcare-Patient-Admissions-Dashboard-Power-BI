# 🏥 Healthcare Patient & Admissions Dashboard — Power BI

## Project Overview

The **Healthcare Patient & Admissions Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI report designed to analyze hospital patient demographics, admission trends, billing, and length of stay across multiple hospitals. The dashboard provides real-time insights into patient volume, medical conditions, and operational KPIs, enabling healthcare administrators and analysts to monitor performance and support data-driven decisions.

## Description

This dashboard transforms raw hospital admission data into a clear, two-page analytical report using Power BI. It helps hospital administrators, healthcare analysts, and decision-makers understand patient demographics, track admission trends over time, and evaluate key operational metrics such as billing and length of stay.

## Tech Stack

- **Power BI Desktop** – Data visualization and dashboard creation
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning, transformation, and preprocessing
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – Measures, KPIs, and calculated columns
- **Data Modeling** – Relationships across Hospital Data, Date, and Medical Condition tables

## Data Source

The dataset contains hospital patient and admission information, including:

- Patient ID, Age, Gender, Blood Type
- Medical Condition, Doctor, Hospital, Room Number
- Admission Type, Admission Date, Length of Stay
- Billing Amount
- Test Results (Normal, Abnormal, Inconclusive)

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Demographics
Provides a snapshot of who is being admitted and why:
- Patient volume by **Age** and **Gender**
- Patient distribution by **Blood Type**
- Admissions by **Medical Condition** and **Hospital**
- Test result breakdown (Normal / Abnormal / Inconclusive)
- Year, Month, and Medical Condition slicers for filtering

### 2. Key Trends
Focuses on how admissions and costs change over time:
- Monthly admission trends by **Admission Type**
- **Average Length of Stay (LOS)** by month
- **Average Billing Amount** by month
- Monthly and daily admission breakdown (pivot view)
- Admission distribution by **LOS Bucket** and **Day Type**
- Hospital-level filtering

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- 🧑‍⚕️ Total Patients
- 🛏️ Room Occupancy
- 💵 Total Billing Amount
- 📈 Average Patient Age
- 👨‍⚕️ Doctors on Record
- ⏱️ Length of Stay (LOS)

## Dashboard Features

The dashboard answers key operational questions such as:

- What is the demographic profile of admitted patients?
- Which medical conditions are most common?
- How do admissions vary by hospital and blood type?
- How has patient volume changed month over month?
- What is the average length of stay and billing amount over time?
- How do admissions differ by day type (weekday/weekend)?

## Business Impact & Insights

The dashboard enables healthcare organizations to:

- Understand patient demographics and case mix
- Identify trends in admissions and resource demand
- Monitor billing and average length of stay over time
- Support staffing and capacity planning
- Improve hospital-level performance comparisons
- Make data-driven operational decisions
